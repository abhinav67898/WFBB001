Types of Design Style

1. Minimalist Style

Clean layouts, generous whitespace, simple typography, and only the elements that matter. Minimalism proves that you don't need more to say more.

2. Neo Brutalist Style

Bold colors, chunky borders, clashing elements, and playful layouts. Neo-brutalism breaks the rules of conventional design and makes the internet feel a little less boring.

3. Maximalist Style

A collision of colors, textures, typography, and imagery. Maximalism embraces visual chaos, turning every corner into an opportunity for expression

4. Brutalist Style

Harsh grids, stark typography, raw compositions, and an intentionally unpolished feel. Brutalism strips design down to its bones and refuses to play nice.
